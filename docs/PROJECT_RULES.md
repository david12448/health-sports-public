# Health and Sports Project Rules

Repository role: PUBLIC presentation and approved normalized data only.

Medical content: use reliable sources, dates, citations and evidence review; clearly distinguish educational information from diagnosis. Urgent red flags must be visible early even when tone is reassuring. Do not promote affiliate products as treatments or make unsupported health claims. Disclose affiliate relationships. Review medical content before publishing.

Sports: prioritize licensed/authorized official results, statistics and source attribution. Do not build betting recommendations or gambling integrations.

Do not expose secrets, personal medical data, unlicensed copyrighted material, or private repository paths/data through the public build.
